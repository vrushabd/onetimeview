Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Create and share a secret link instantly without signing up.

How many times can a link be viewed?

You choose 1–10 views in the creator. Quick create defaults to 1 view.

Are files supported?

Yes. Images, videos, and documents up to 100MB free or 500MB on premium.

Can I add a password?

Absolutely. Both the quick creator and advanced creator allow you to add a password for viewing.
